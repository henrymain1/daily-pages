Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Daily Pages is a personal journaling and daily-planning app. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. It is written plainly on purpose — there is no third-party advertising, tracking, or data selling of any kind.

What the app stores

When you create an account, your entries, to-do lists, and planner data are stored in your own account in our database provider (Supabase) and are protected by row-level security so that only you can read them. Your journal content is never used for advertising and is never sold or shared.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you choose to connect it, Daily Pages uses Google's Calendar API only to:

  • Read your calendar events for a given day so they can be shown alongside your planner schedule.
  • Create and update calendar events that you create in Daily Pages (for example, a to-do deadline), including a reminder so your device notifies you.
  • Delete those same events when you remove them in Daily Pages.

The app only touches events; it never reads or changes your calendar settings, sharing, or other Google account data. It does not read events it did not create for any purpose other than displaying them back to you in your own planner. Daily Pages' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your Google authorization is stored securely on the server and is used only to make the calendar requests described above. You can disconnect at any time from the app's menu, which revokes the authorization and deletes the stored token. Nothing about your Google Calendar is ever sold, shared, or used for advertising.

What we don't do

  • No advertising networks or ad tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No analytics profiling of your journal content.

Deleting your data

You can delete your entries at any time within the app. To have your entire account and all associated data removed, contact us at the email below and it will be deleted.
